Patch Panic

A cable puzzle game for Android.

Jump-start ambulances, silence screaming car alarms and get the power back on before the fuse runs out. Drag the right cable to the right terminal, in the right order: red plus on first, black minus off first. Every five levels a new story chapter begins, and every day brings a new emergency call.

Patch Panic is in development and not yet on the Play Store.
