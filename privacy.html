Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The Patch Panic game

Patch Panic saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) only on your device. The current version does not collect, send or share any personal data, and it has no accounts.

Future versions may show ads through Google AdMob, offer in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, and use anonymous gameplay analytics. If they do, Google may use your device's advertising ID as described in Google's partner policy, and this page will be updated before that version is released.

The Patch Panic APK Uploader

"Patch Panic APK Uploader" is a Google sign-in used only by the developer to upload test builds of the game to the developer's own Google Drive folder. It does not access, store or share data from anyone else's Google account.

Children

Patch Panic does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ChanakaMadhuranga/patch-panic-site.
